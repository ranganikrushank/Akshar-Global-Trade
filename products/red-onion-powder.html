About Red Onion Powder

Our Red Onion Powder is crafted from carefully selected red onions known for their vibrant color and robust flavor profile. The onions are dehydrated using advanced technology that preserves their natural pigments, intense aroma, and nutritional value.

Red onion powder is particularly valued for its ability to add both flavor and natural color to food products. The anthocyanins present in red onions provide antioxidant properties and a distinctive purple-red hue that enhances the visual appeal of dishes.

Key Advantages

  • Natural food coloring agent
  • Rich in antioxidants (anthocyanins)
  • More pungent than white onion powder
  • Adds visual appeal to dishes
  • Convenient and shelf-stable
  • No artificial colors or additives
