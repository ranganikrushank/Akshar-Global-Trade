About Banana Powder

Bananas are one of the most popular fruits worldwide, known for their delicious taste and numerous health benefits. However, fresh bananas have a relatively short shelf life, which limits their export potential. This is where dehydrated banana powder comes into play. This product retains all the essential nutrients by converting fresh bananas into powder form while significantly extending the shelf life, making it a highly sought-after ingredient in various industries.

Dehydrated banana powder is made by grinding ripe bananas into a fine powder. This process preserves the natural sweetness and nutritional content of the bananas, making it a versatile ingredient used in a variety of applications. From baby food and dietary supplements to baked goods and smoothies, dehydrated banana powder adds flavor, nutrition, and texture to a wide range of products.

Premium quality is ensured by sourcing the finest bananas and processing them under hygienic conditions. Advanced dehydration techniques maintain consistency, remove moisture, and preserve natural flavor, aroma, and nutrients without any additives or preservatives.

Dehydrated banana powder is widely used across industries such as food and beverages, health and wellness, cosmetics, and even animal feed. It enhances product quality while offering natural sweetness and nutritional value.

It is rich in potassium, which supports heart health and blood pressure control. It also contains dietary fiber for digestion, antioxidants for immunity, and natural sugars that act as an energy booster, making it ideal for health-conscious consumers.

With its long shelf life, versatility, and health benefits, banana powder has become an essential ingredient for global markets and export businesses.

Our premium Banana Powder is made from carefully selected ripe bananas that are peeled, sliced, and dehydrated using advanced spray drying or freeze drying technology. This process preserves the natural nutrients, flavor, and color of fresh bananas.

Banana powder is an excellent source of potassium, dietary fiber, vitamins (especially B6 and C), and natural sugars. It's easily digestible, making it ideal for baby food, health supplements, and sports nutrition products. The powder dissolves easily in liquids and adds natural sweetness to various food applications.
