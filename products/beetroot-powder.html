About BeetRoot Powder

Our premium BeetRoot Powder is made from fresh, organic beetroots that are carefully washed, sliced, and dehydrated using low-temperature drying methods to preserve the vibrant red color, nutrients, and beneficial compounds. The dried beets are then ground into a fine, vibrant powder.

Beetroot powder is renowned for its high nitrate content, which converts to nitric oxide in the body, helping to improve blood flow, lower blood pressure, and enhance athletic performance. It's also rich in betalains (powerful antioxidants), folate, manganese, and vitamin C, making it a true superfood.
